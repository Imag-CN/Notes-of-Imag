>[!problem] Problem 1
>During the class we proved that $L/K$ being finite Galois implies that
>$$ L \otimes_K L \cong L^{\oplus d} $$
>as $K$-algebras. Now prove that the converse is true.

**Proof:**
Reduced $\Rightarrow$ separable. Use primitive element argument.

>[!problem] Problem 2
>Try to find conditions on $L \otimes_K L$ corresponding to $L/K$ being finite normal.

**Proof:**
$$
L \otimes_K L \cong (L[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^{n_{i}})) ^{\oplus d}
$$
For some $n_{1},\dots,n_{d}\in \mathbb{N}$.
