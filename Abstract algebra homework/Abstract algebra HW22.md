> [!problem] Problem 1
> Write $\cos\frac{2\pi}{13}$ in terms of successive radicals of rational numbers.

**Proof:**
$$
\cos\frac{2\pi}{13} = \frac12\Bigg(\sqrt[3]{\frac{-1+\sqrt{13} + \sqrt{-26+6\sqrt{13}}}{2}} 
+ \sqrt[3]{\frac{-1+\sqrt{13} - \sqrt{-26+6\sqrt{13}}}{2}} \Bigg),
$$

> [!problem] Problem 2
> Let $P(x)\in\mathbb{Q}[x]$ be an irreducible polynomial of degree $n$. Prove that $P(x)$ has no multiple roots in $\mathbb{C}$.

**Proof:**
Since $P'(x)\neq 0$ and $P(x)$ irreducible, $P(x)$ and $P'(x)$ has no common divisors in $\mathbb{Q}[x]$. Therefore, they have no common divisors in $\mathbb{C}[x]$, so $P(x)$ has no multiple roots in $\mathbb{C}$.
