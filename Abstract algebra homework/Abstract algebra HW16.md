All rings are commutative with unit.

>[!problem] Problem 1
>Let $R$ be a ring. Find the conditions on $R$ so that there is a natural bijection between
>
>$$
>\{ \text{homomorphisms } \varphi : \mathbb{Q}[x,y] \to R \} \leftrightarrow R^2
>$$

**Proof:** $R$ is a $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra.
___

>[!problem] Problem 2
>Consider the ring homomorphism
>$$
>\begin{aligned}
>\varphi : \mathbb{Q}[x,y] &\to \mathbb{Q}(t) \\
>x &\mapsto \frac{2t}{1+t^2} \\
>y &\mapsto \frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}
>\end{aligned}
>$$
>Find the ideal $\ker\varphi$.

**Proof:** $(x^{2}+y^{2}-1)$.
___

>[!problem] Problem 3
>Let $\mathcal{C}$ be the category of rings. Prove that the zero ring is the final object in $\mathcal{C}$, and $\mathbb{Z}$ is the initial object in $\mathcal{C}$.

**Proof:** Trivial.
___

>[!problem] Problem 4
>Find all ideals of the following rings:
>(i) $\mathbb{Z}$;
>(ii) $\mathbb{R}$;
>(iii) $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ where $n$ is a positive integer number;
>(iv) $\mathbb{C}[x]$;
>(v) $\mathbb{R}[x]$;
>(vi) $\mathbb{Q}[x]$.

**Proof:**
**(i)** $\{ (n):n\in \mathbb{Z} \}$.
**(ii)** $\{ (0),(1) \}$.
**(iii)** $\{ (d):d\mid n\}$.
**(iv)(v)(vi)** $\{ (p(x)):p(x)\in \mathbb{F}(x) \}$ where $\mathbb{F}$ is the field.
