> [!problem] Problem 1 ()
> (Krasner's lemma) Let E/K be a finite Galois extension of a complete field K. Prolong the valuation of K to E. Let $x \in E$ and let $\{x_1, \dots, x_n\}$ be the set of conjugates of $x$ over K, with $x = x_1$. Let $y \in E$ be such that $\|y - x\| < \|y - x_i\|$ for $i \ge 2$. Show that $x$ belongs to the field $K(y)$. (Note that if $x_i$ is conjugate to $x$ over $K(y)$, then $\|y - x\| = \|y - x_i\|$, according to cor. 3.)
