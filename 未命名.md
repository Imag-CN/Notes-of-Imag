>[!definition] Definition 2
>Take $p$ a prime number, then the extended ideal $p \mathcal{O}_K$ is an ideal in a Dedekind domain $\mathcal{O}_{K}$, so we have
>$$
>p \mathcal{O}_K = \mathfrak{P}_1^{e_1} \mathfrak{P}_2^{e_2} \cdots \mathfrak{P}_g^{e_g}
>$$
>Here, $\mathfrak{P}_i$ are distinct prime ideals in $\mathcal{O}_K$, and $e_i$ are positive integers called the **ramification indices**.
>
>*   The prime $p$ is said to be **unramified** in $K$ if $e_i = 1$ for all $i$, and the residue fields $\mathcal{O}_K / \mathfrak{P}_i$ are separable over the finite field $\mathbb{F}_p$.
>*   The prime $p$ is said to be **ramified** in $K$ if $e_i > 1$ for at least one $i$.
>
>Essentially, ramification occurs when a prime number in $\mathbb{Q}$ "splits" in $K$ with multiplicity.

>[!theorem] Theorem 2 (Dedekind)
>A prime $p$ ramifies in $K$ if and only if $p$ divides $\Delta_K$.

>[!definition] Definition 3 Inertia Group 
Let $L/K$ be a **Galois** extension of number fields with Galois group $G = \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$.

Let $\mathfrak{P}$ be a prime ideal of the ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_L$ lying above a prime ideal $\mathfrak{p}$ of $\mathcal{O}_K$. So $\mathfrak{p} = \mathfrak{P} \cap K$.

The **decomposition group** $D_{\mathfrak{P}} = D_{\mathfrak{P}}(\mathfrak{P}/\mathfrak{p})$ is the subgroup of $G$ that stabilizes $\mathfrak{P}$:

$$
D_{\mathfrak{P}} = \{ \sigma \in G \mid \sigma(\mathfrak{P}) = \mathfrak{P} \}.
$$

Consider the residue field extensions: $l = \mathcal{O}_L / \mathfrak{P}$ and $k = \mathcal{O}_K / \mathfrak{p}$.
The action of $D_{\mathfrak{P}}$ on $L$ induces an action on the residue field $l$, giving a homomorphism
$$
\varphi: D_{\mathfrak{P}} \to \operatorname{Gal}(l/k)
$$
which is surjective.

The kernel of this homomorphism is called the **inertia group** $I_{\mathfrak{P}} = I_{\mathfrak{P}}(\mathfrak{P}/\mathfrak{p})$:

$$
I_{\mathfrak{P}} = \ker \varphi = \{ \sigma \in D_{\mathfrak{P}} \mid \sigma(\alpha) \equiv \alpha \ (\mathrm{mod} \ \mathfrak{P}) \ \text{ for all } \alpha \in \mathcal{O}_L \}.
$$

**Properties:**
1.  The inertia group $I_{\mathfrak{P}}$ is normal in $D_{\mathfrak{P}}$.
2.  The order of the inertia group equals the ramification index $e = e(\mathfrak{P}/\mathfrak{p})$.
3.  A prime $\mathfrak{p}$ is **unramified** in $L/K$ if and only if the inertia group $I_{\mathfrak{P}}$ is trivial for all primes $\mathfrak{P}$ above $\mathfrak{p}$.
4.  The quotient $D_{\mathfrak{P}} / I_{\mathfrak{P}}$ is isomorphic to $\operatorname{Gal}(l/k)$ (a cyclic group, generated by the Frobenius automorphism when $l/k$ is finite).
5.  The inertia group $I_{\mathfrak{P}}$ is a $p$-group, where $p$ is the residue characteristic.

Essentially, the decomposition group $D_{\mathfrak{P}}$ captures the "local symmetry" of the prime $\mathfrak{P}$, and the inertia subgroup $I_{\mathfrak{P}}$ consists of those symmetries that act trivially on the residue classes. The non-triviality of $I_{\mathfrak{P}}$ precisely measures the ramification at $\mathfrak{P}$.
