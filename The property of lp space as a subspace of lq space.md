For $1\leq p < q\leq \infty$, it is obvious that $l^{p}$ space is a subspace of $l^{q}$ space. We shall investigate some property of this subspace.

>[!problem] Topology
>Consider the inclusion map: $f: l^{p}\to l^{q}$. Then
>
>1. $f$ is continous; 
>2. $f$ is not a closed mapping
>3. $f$ is an open mapping
